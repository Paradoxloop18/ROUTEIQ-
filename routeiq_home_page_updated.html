RouteIQ Android Mobile App

Play Store & PWA Ready

RouteIQ operates as a progressive mobile application on any Android smartphone. Drivers, students, and coordinators can install and use it directly without requiring external hardware.

📲 Method 1: Instant Install on Android (Chrome / Samsung Internet)

  1. Open this website in Google Chrome on your Android phone.
  2. Tap the browser menu (three dots ⋮ in the top right).
  3. Tap "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  4. RouteIQ will install directly to your app drawer like a native Play Store app!

🏪 Method 2: Package for Google Play Store (APK / AAB)

This app contains standard manifest.json and sw.js Service Worker. You can convert it into a signed Google Play Store APK/AAB in 2 minutes:

1. Visit: https://www.pwabuilder.com
2. Enter URL & Download Android Package (.apk / .aab)
3. Upload directly to Google Play Console!
